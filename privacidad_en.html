Privacy Policy — Tinta y Sueños

Last updated: 30/10/2026

"Tinta y Sueños" (the "Game") is a free video game by Semper Animo Studios ("we"). This policy explains what information is used when you play.

1. What we do NOT do

The Game does not ask you to create an account, or for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location. It has no chat or user-generated content, and we do not sell your data.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game stores on your own device, without sending it to any server of ours: language, volume, high score, zone progress and the enemies you have discovered (Bestiary).

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: an occasional ad after you lose and, if you choose, a video to continue. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), ad interaction data, crash and performance data and approximate location derived from the IP address, to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. More information: how Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services.

  • iOS: before personalizing ads, the Game asks for your permission through App Tracking Transparency. If you say "No", you will see non-personalized ads and the Game works the same. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • European Economic Area, UK and other regions: a consent form (Google UMP) is shown where required by law, and you can change your choice later.
  • We request ads suitable for general audiences.

3 bis. Game Center

The Game uses Apple Game Center for achievements and leaderboards. It is optional and only works if the player is signed in to Game Center. Your scores and achievements are sent to Apple, which handles them under its privacy policy. We do not receive your name or email.

4. Children


(a) The Game is for a general audience and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

5. Your rights

Since we do not hold your personal information, there is no data we can look up or delete; for ad data you can limit tracking in your device settings or with the consent form. If you have questions, contact us.

6. Changes

If we change this policy we will post the new version on this page with its date.
